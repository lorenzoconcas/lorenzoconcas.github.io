Who is responsible

The controller is Lorenzo Concas (independent developer). For any privacy request write to lore.appstore@outlook.com.

In short

MiniTV does not collect, store or share personal data. There is no account, and we use no tracking, analytics or advertising. We run no servers that receive the app’s data.

What stays on your device

The sources you add (playlist and channel addresses), playlists imported from files, loaded channels, order, hidden channels, customizations (name, logo, colors) and preferences are saved only on your device.

iCloud sync (iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV)

If you are signed in to iCloud, MiniTV can sync your library between your devices using iCloud key-value storage, which belongs to your Apple account. What is synced: the addresses of remote playlists and single channels (including any HTTP headers or cookies you entered for a channel), names, the order of channels and groups, hidden channels, customizations, the TV guide address and identifier of single channels, and which sources you turned off. Playlists imported from files stay on the device and are not synced.

We do not receive this data and cannot read it: it only travels between your own devices and is handled by Apple under its privacy policy. Sync is on by default and can be turned off in Sources → Sync with iCloud. To delete data already stored in iCloud use your device’s iCloud settings (Manage Storage → MiniTV). Avoid entering passwords or sensitive cookies in a channel’s headers if you don’t want them in your iCloud.

Network connections

The app only connects to the addresses you provide or contained in the playlists you add (playlists, audio and video streams, channel images, TV guide files). Online playlists are downloaded again from time to time to keep them up to date. Those servers receive your IP address and any headers you set, and are outside our control. Using third-party content is the user’s responsibility.

Links from some video services open in the service’s own player, embedded in the app: that service may use its own cookies under its privacy policy.

TV guide (iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV, from version 3.0)

MiniTV includes no listings data. If a playlist or a channel provides a guide address, the app downloads the file from that address, refreshes it from time to time and keeps a temporary copy on the device. The server hosting it receives an ordinary network request (including your IP address). Nothing is sent to us, not even which programmes you look up.

In-app purchases

Payments are handled by Apple: we never see your payment details or identity. The purchase status (MiniTV Premium) is verified on the device through StoreKit.

Android

The Android version does not use iCloud or sync data: everything stays on the device.
