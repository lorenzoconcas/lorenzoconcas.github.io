In short

TravelBuddy has no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking or servers of its own. The developer does not receive and cannot read your trips, documents or photos.

What stays on your device

Trips, stops, documents, tickets, expenses, memories, photos and covers. Text in documents is read on your device, with Apple Intelligence where available. Reminders, widgets and the Lock Screen countdown are created on the device.

iCloud sync

With iCloud on, your data and files sync through the private database of your iCloud account, processed by Apple under its privacy policy. We have no access to it.

Permissions

Camera (document scanning), photos (memories and covers, suggested on the device), write-only calendar access and notifications are used only for the features you choose. The app does not ask for your device’s location.

Data sent over the network

Weather: to show the forecast, the app sends Apple Weather the coordinates of your trip location (the current or next stop), rounded to about 1 km. They are not linked to your identity. Maps and place search: requests go to Apple (MapKit). Suggested covers: when you choose a place, the app sends its name and coordinates to Wikimedia servers (Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons), which may log your IP address under their own policy.

In-app purchases

Apple handles payments: we never see your payment details or your identity. TravelBuddy Pro is verified on the device through StoreKit. For the free trip limit the app stores only random identifiers, in your iCloud and Keychain.

Retention and deletion

Data stays as long as you keep it. Deleting a trip removes the copies of its files, on the device and in iCloud; your originals in Files or Photos are not touched. To delete everything, delete your trips, then the app’s data in iCloud settings, and uninstall the app.

Changes
